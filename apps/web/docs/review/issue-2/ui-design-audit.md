User constraints take precedence over these tips. The implemented app still fails the requested Blue direction: C/N/R/S contain pink, and R adds a decorative source gradient. Implemented S/L place route content directly on gray. A removes that source decoration and places reader/saved/settings content within white surfaces. A's `.route-surface` matches the supplied `templates/radiant.zip` login surface (`rounded-xl bg-white shadow-md ring-1 ring-black/5`, `p-7 sm:p-11`); its saved source/title/date order follows `templates/spotlight.zip` articles. Those archive members were inspected read-only. Product controls reuse installed `src/components/ui/{button,input,alert}.tsx`; these facts do not mean A has been integrated into the product.

Pending priorities: **P1** integrate the already selected A composition and blue palette, then verify all routes/states; **P1** show meaningful action labels, including save-and-advance and defer-to-tail; **P1** improve small-text contrast and non-color active-state cues. Declared implemented secondary colors produce approximately 2.70–3.49:1 against their backgrounds; screenshots are not a formal accessibility test. **P2** display existing keyboard shortcuts near actions. No login, chart, pricing, template-onboarding, decorative media, or tooltip system is proposed merely to satisfy unrelated advice.

This audit changes documentation only. It does not claim all tips pass, test real feeds, or validate every device, focus path, zoom level, or assistive technology.
